Ricochet Foundry – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026

Ricochet Foundry ("the game") is made by an independent developer based in Çanakkale, Türkiye ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. The game has no accounts: we never ask for your name, email address or any other contact details.

1. Information stored only on your device

Your settings (sound, music, vibration, language), your records, achievements, unlocked colors, the run you are playing and whether you bought "Remove Ads" are saved on your device. This data is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos (for example "Revive" or "2x Gold") and occasional full-screen ads between battles. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID or Apple's IDFA), IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws, a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform is shown before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time.
  • On iOS, the App Tracking Transparency prompt asks for permission before the IDFA is used. If you decline, ads are not personalized with it.
  • You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.

Learn how Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the game is played and to fix crashes. They collect anonymous gameplay events (for example runs started, waves reached, battles won or lost, ads watched, purchases made), an app instance identifier, device model, operating system version, app version and crash logs. This data does not identify you by name and is used only to improve the game. See firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

4. Purchases

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive your payment details; the store only tells the game that the purchase was made so it can be restored.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Data retention and your rights

Analytics data is kept by Firebase for up to 14 months. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Türkiye's KVKK) you may have the right to access, correct or delete data about you, or to object to its processing. Because we do not know who you are, please include your device's app instance ID or advertising ID if you contact us, and we will forward deletion requests to Google.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.

8. Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/cngzkrs/ricochet-foundry/issues.
